How to decide. These steps are the same ones the coded rule layer follows. Work through them in order, and give a level whenever a profile matches.
1. Prerequisites. The age must be explicit and between 18 and 120, and the record must not contain a contradiction listed in the decision checks. If either fails, answer needs_review.
2. Matching profiles. Use only what the patient actually reported. A profile matches when all of its all conditions hold, at least one condition in each any group holds, no concept in its none list is reported, and the patient meets any min_age. A condition that requires the state absent needs an explicit denial; every other condition needs a positive report. A concept the record never mentions is simply missing: it neither satisfies nor blocks a profile unless that profile explicitly requires it. Do not match a similar or more serious-sounding presentation, and do not treat known medical history alone as a new symptom.
3. Decision A. If any A profile matches, answer 1. Otherwise, if an A danger signal is reported, or the patient’s words make it explicitly uncertain (including uncertain breathing), answer needs_review.
4. Decision B. If any B profile matches, answer 2; other symptoms or danger signals do not change this. Otherwise, if a B danger signal is reported or explicitly uncertain in the patient’s words, answer needs_review, except that severe_pain does not stop the process when limb_injury is reported, and chest_discomfort does not stop the process when both cough and fever are reported.
5. Decision C. If one or more C profiles match, combine their expected_resource_types: zero types gives 5, one gives 4, and two or more gives 3. These resource types are fixed research hypotheses; tests, devices and pain scores never change the count. A C answer is blocked only by these checks: (a) unmapped_symptom is reported, or explicitly uncertain in the patient’s words, or head_injury is reported or explicitly uncertain and no matched profile accounts for it; (b) pregnancy is reported with abdominal pain or burning urination; (c) a reported concept in c_symptoms_needing_coverage is not covered by any matched profile in c_accounted_by_profile (a covered general concept also covers its specific forms in c_accounting_includes_specific_forms); or (d) worsening is reported and every matched profile has zero resource types. Minor accompanying findings that are not in c_symptoms_needing_coverage, such as bruising, dry or peeling skin, local redness, stiffness, local warmth, congestion, bloating, dry mouth or tenderness, never block a matched profile. If no C profile matches, answer needs_review; never default to 3.
6. Commit to the answer these steps give. needs_review is correct only for the reasons named in steps 1 to 5. A sign the patient never mentioned, such as whether they hit their head, have chest pain or have trouble breathing, is not a reason to abstain: it is recorded as unassessed, and the prediction stands.
